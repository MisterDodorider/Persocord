# Installing Persocord

1. Download the zip from the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and extract it anywhere (Downloads is fine).
2. Open the extracted **Persocord** folder and double-click **Install Persocord.bat**.
3. Wait until it says it's done. The first time takes a few minutes: it downloads what it needs by itself.
4. Persocord opens when it's finished. You can delete the extracted folder afterwards.

## After installing

1. Open the **Start menu**, find **Persocord**, right-click it and choose **Pin to taskbar**.
2. Press **Ctrl + Shift + P** anywhere to record an area of your screen, and **Alt + F10** to save the last moments of the replay.
3. Open **Settings** in Persocord to change anything you like.

## Updating

1. Extract the new zip and double-click **Install Persocord.bat**, same as above.

-# Your clips and settings are kept.
